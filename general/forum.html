 Online Parent Support Forum

This forum enables you to read comments and observations from other infant botulism parents and to post your questions and comments if you would like to do so.

How to register
When calling up the IBTPP Forum in your browser, you will see a menu across the top which should have images for "Home", "Help", "Search", "Login", and "Register". Click the link/image that says "register". You will be taken to a screen where you can select a user name. Then you will be prompted to enter your desired user name and a valid email address.

If your desired use name is available, you will receive an email with a temporary password - which will allow you to login into the forum and make posts.



Or you can also visit our Facebook page that has been set up for families to connect with each other. You can search infant botulism on Facebook or click here.

For non-urgent questions and comments, please email
